01 — The Company

More than twenty-four years of carrying the goods that keep Zambia moving.

Panafrica Distributors Limited (PAD Ltd) is a Zambian-registered distributor of fast-moving consumer goods, established over 24 years ago and owned by Hon. Suresh M. Desai and Rajen Desai, both Zambian citizens. It is not a conventional wholesaler, but a specialist in the last mile.

We operate out of a 3,000 m² warehouse and office facility at Plot 20108, Mumbwa Road, Chinika Area, Lusaka, supported by branches in Monze (1,000 m²) and Livingstone (2,000 m²). Our model is Direct Store Delivery: our own people, in our own vehicles, placing and replenishing stock on the shelf where it actually sells.

02 — The Network

Three depots. Four provinces. One last mile.

Our presence spans the Lusaka, Western, Eastern and Southern Provinces. Lusaka is the hub for the capital and Western Province, while Monze and Livingstone carry the Southern Province, with weekly routes into the towns and townships that national chains rarely reach.
