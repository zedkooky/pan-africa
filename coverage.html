Coverage

Four provinces. Weekly. On the shelf.

From the Chinika Area head office in Lusaka and branches in Monze and Livingstone, we service about 8,000 outlets every week across the Lusaka, Western, Eastern and Southern Provinces — supermarkets and hotels in the city, bars, clubs and small shops in the townships.

The Network

Three depots. Four provinces. One last mile.

Our presence spans the Lusaka, Western, Eastern and Southern Provinces. Lusaka is the hub for the capital and Western Province, while Monze and Livingstone carry the Southern Province, with weekly routes into the towns and townships that national chains rarely reach.

Where the last mile reaches

An indicative map of the network. Each route line is drawn hub by hub — Lusaka west to Mongu, east along the Great East Road through Petauke to Katete and Chipata, and south through Monze and Choma to Livingstone and the Kazungula border.

  • DepotsLusaka (HQ), Monze and Livingstone — the three hubs that hold stock and dispatch the fleet.
